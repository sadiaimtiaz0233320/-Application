Questions

Before you start

What do I need to enter?
Plot length and width in feet, orientation, number of floors, a room count and a budget. Everything has a sensible default, so you can generate a plan with one click.
Can I edit the generated plan?
Yes. In the 2D viewer drag rooms to move them and pull the corner handles to resize. Walls, dimensions and the bill of quantities update to match.
Which files can I download?
A PNG and an SVG of the floor plan, an OBJ of the 3D model, a 3D snapshot and a print-ready A4 sheet that includes the bill of quantities.
How are cost figures calculated?
From built-up area, floors and the cost mix of the style you choose. Treat the numbers as planning estimates, not a quotation.
What is ArchiBot?
A rule-based assistant, bottom right. It reads your current project and answers questions about daylight, vastu, budget and solar.
Does it work on a phone?
The layout adapts to small screens. Plan editing and 3D orbit work with touch, and the walkthrough switches to drag-to-look.
